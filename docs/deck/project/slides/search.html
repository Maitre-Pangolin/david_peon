03–04 · Search and shortlist

The brief: iconic and funny, under five seconds

The search script listed every sentence of 5 s or less, and every pair of back-to-back sentences: 549 options.

Claude read them all, ran keyword hunts for phrases inside longer lines, and proposed 80 candidates. It flagged 6 that might not be his voice.

My feedback on Claude’s first shortlist: search more, allow lines up to 5 seconds, and aim for iconic and funny. That one sentence of feedback reshaped the search: Claude added two-sentence pairs (that’s how “Termites. Not easy to catch when you’re blind.” turned up) and re-read everything.
