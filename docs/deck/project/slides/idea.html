The idea

Swap the Warcraft peon for Sir David Attenborough

peon-ping plays a short voice line whenever Claude Code starts, finishes, fails or needs you. A pack is just WAV files plus a JSON manifest mapping them to 7 events.

Real clips only

Every line is something he actually said. No AI cloning of a real person’s voice.

Clips, not programmes

Only 1–5 s lines get shared, credited to the BBC. The full-length downloads stay on the laptop.

Official sources

10 uploads from the BBC and BBC Earth YouTube channels. Impressions and fan edits left out.

The first thing Claude did was look at how peon-ping packs are built on my machine, then propose a plan. It raised the rights question unprompted, and we agreed ground rules before touching anything: real clips only and official sources only. We started with personal use only, then decided at the end to publish the 52 short clips, credited to the BBC.
