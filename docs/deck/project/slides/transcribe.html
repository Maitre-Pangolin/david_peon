02 · Transcribe

Speech-to-text ran on the laptop

  • Whisper large-v3-turbo (OpenAI, open weights), the MLX build for Apple Silicon
  • 1.6 GB model, downloaded once. No audio sent anywhere
  • 2 h 40 of audio transcribed in about 12 minutes
  • A timestamp on every word, so a line can be cut to the hundredth of a second

Gotcha

Whisper invents words over music

Over music-only passages it wrote “I’m sorry.”, 20 times in a row in one clip. The fix: drop segments Whisper itself flags as repetitive (compression ratio above 2.4), plus a short list of known phantom phrases.

Before downloading the model, Claude explained what it is and how much disk it needs, and checked I had space (100 GB free). The phantom “I’m sorry.” was caught in a quality check on the transcripts, not by me listening.
